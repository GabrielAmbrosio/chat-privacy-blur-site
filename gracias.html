Thank you for your purchase!

Chat Privacy Blur Pro is yours. To activate it:

  1. Find your license key

    Polar shows it in the purchase confirmation and emails it to you. You can also see it in your Polar customer portal.

  2. Open the extension

    Click the Chat Privacy Blur icon in the browser toolbar (if you don't see it, click the puzzle piece and pin it).

  3. Paste the key and click Activate

    At the bottom of the popup, under "Chat Privacy Blur Pro", paste the key into "License key" and click Activate. Pro features unlock instantly.

Your license

You can activate it on up to 3 devices. If you switch computers, use Remove from this device in the popup to free a slot.
